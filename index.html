Check that you are on headphones

The modulation and the stereo placement only work properly if each ear gets its own channel. This is a short listening test from a published method paper: three tones, and you pick the quietest. Six trials, about a minute. It is optional and it changes nothing about the sound.

Runs entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is nothing to sign up for.

Not a medical device and not a treatment. The research behind this is modest, mixed, and summarised honestly in the README, including the parts that argue against it. If attention is causing real problems in your life, talk to a clinician.
